The problem

Getting the diagnosis right is not guaranteed.

46.1%diagnostic accuracy among health workers in Nigeria's public health facilities in 2025. It was 56.2% in 2023.
Secondary facilities68.1%

Where it hurts

Primary health centres scored 44.6%, against 68.1% at secondary facilities.

Who it affects

Doctors scored 74.3%. Nurses and midwives scored 56.3%, and community health workers 43.7%.

What we are doing

ActiCheck puts a structured second look in the pocket of the person at the bedside, even with no signal.

Source: National Health Facility Survey 2025, National Bureau of Statistics, as reported by the News Agency of Nigeria. Accuracy was measured with case scenarios for five conditions: diarrhoea, pneumonia, malaria, postpartum haemorrhage and birth asphyxia. Read the report coverage.

How it works

From first symptom to next step.

Four steps, in the same order as the app. The phone below is a preview of the real screens. It plays on its own and is not clickable.

Step 1Open a file, add vitals

Use initials or a bed number, never a full name. Enter whatever you measured.

Step 2Add symptoms and signs

Tick what the patient reports and what you see.

Step 3See the possibilities

Illnesses are ranked by how well they fit, with danger signs shown first.

Step 4Examine and update

Mark each finding Yes or No and the list re-ranks. Then hand the case over by QR code.
